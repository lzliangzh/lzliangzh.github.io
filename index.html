Research Interests

  • LLM Interpretability. My recent work centers on Interpretability for post-training, and as a method for (Chinese) linguistics. I have contributed to a parallel Chinese compound–phrase dataset and a multi-dimensional probing framework (EMNLP 2026), the first operational test of whether compounds and phrases follow the same combinatorial principles in Chinese.

  • Agent System. I have contributed to a structured representation for agent skills (ICLR 2027, under review), which turns text-only skill documents into artifacts that machines can route and execute.

I am also interested in a broad range of promising research topics.22 I’m a very beginner of Finance (Quanting). I’m also very interested in the field of Robotics and Embodied AI. If you have any ideas or thoughts to share, I am enthusiastic about in-depth discussions and potential collaboration.
